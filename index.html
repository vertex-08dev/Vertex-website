Clear Terms

Do you charge monthly maintenance fees?

No. We build independent, high-performance assets. Once delivered, the codebase is 100% yours. We don't believe in holding your operation hostage with recurring tech fees.

What happens if a bug appears after launch?

Every full project includes a strict 15-day Stabilization Period. If any visual discrepancy or performance drop occurs within this window, we correct it immediately at zero cost.

Can I request new features later?

Absolutely. We treat future updates—like adding a new page or a new 3D element—as independent micro-projects. You only pay for exactly what you need, exactly when you need it.

How do the standalone components work?

When you purchase from 'The Vault', you receive the raw HTML, CSS, and JS files, perfectly formatted and ready to be integrated into your existing project or CMS.
